## Methodology

### Scoring framework & weights

| Pillar | Weight | How it is scored |
|---|---|---|
| Career opportunities | 40% | Mean of 12 sub-scores (agencies, performance, e-commerce, startups, scaleups, international firms, HQs, networking, promotion, entrepreneurship, employer mobility, market size) |
| Disposable income | 25% | Net salary − rent − essentials, in DKK, across seniority levels |
| Cost of living | 15% | Absolute rent + essentials and rent-to-income burden (lower = higher score) |
| Lifestyle | 10% | Climate, safety, healthcare, transport, bikeability, green space, air, international community |
| Economic stability | 5% | Unemployment, inflation, growth, resilience, funding environment |
| English friendliness | 5% | EF EPI + realism of an English-only marketing career |
